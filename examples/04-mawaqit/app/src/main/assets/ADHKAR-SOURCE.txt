adhkar.json: where the adhkar come from
========================================

No Arabic in adhkar.json was typed by hand. Every Arabic string is copied from one of the three openly licensed
datasets below, all of which take their text from "Hisn al-Muslim" (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sheikh Sa'id ibn
Wahf al-Qahtani, or (the du'as from the Quran) from the Tanzil Quran text itself. Build it again with:

  python3 -I tools/adhkar_build.py <Seen-Arabic dir> <fitrahive dir> <muslim_db.db> app/src/main/assets/adhkar.json <quran dir>
  python3 -I tools/adhkar_crosscheck.py app/src/main/assets/adhkar.json <reference dir> app/src/main/java/dev/framio/mawaqit/core/Texts.kt <quran dir>

<quran dir> holds ayah_<sura>_<ayah>.json for the 19 ayat below, each
  https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/ayah/<sura>:<ayah>/editions/quran-simple,quran-simple-clean,en.pickthall


1. Morning (أذكار الصباح) and evening (أذكار المساء): 26 and 24 items, ids "me<order>"
   Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB, files ar.json + en.json, commit 29d7623fede52eca835a789025dfda866e8cfe44
   https://github.com/Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB
   Arabic, count, English translation and transliteration, virtue (fadl, Arabic and English), source (Arabic and
   English). Its own sources: hisnmuslim.com, sunnah.com, Hisn al-Muslim and its sharh (al-Qahtani), Rawa' al-Atshaa'
   (al-Muqaddim).
   License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2024 Seen Arabic

2. After the prayer (الأذكار بعد السلام من الصلاة): 13 of the 15 items, ids "ap<n>"
   fitrahive/dua-dhikr, data/dua-dhikr/dhikr-after-salah/en.json, commit f42f895f914319a844c3e3c2279483cae060ea19
   https://github.com/fitrahive/dua-dhikr
   Arabic, transliteration ("latin"), translation, count (from "notes": "Read 33x"), benefits, source.
   Also the transliterations of before-sleep / upon-waking items whose Arabic is the same text (daily-dua/en.json).
   License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2023 Fitrahive

3. Before sleep (أذكار النوم, Hisn chapter 29), upon waking (أذكار الاستيقاظ, chapter 1) and 2 after-prayer items
   (hm59 "Allahumma a'inni…", hm72 "…yuhyi wa yumit…" ×10): ids "hm<item id>"
   my-prayers/muslim-data, assets/db/muslim_db_v2.7.0.db (tables azkar_*), muslim-data-flutter commit
   6df43672a5306ff6e19b1e29dacfe88cf6385ccb
   https://github.com/my-prayers/muslim-data-flutter
   Arabic, English translation, reference.
   License: Apache License 2.0, Copyright 2025 Kosrat Dlshad Ahmed (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

4. The Quran: the du'as of the "quran" collection (15 items, ids "q<sura>_<first ayah>"): 2:127, 2:201, 3:8, 3:16,
   3:147, 7:23, 14:40-41, 17:24, 18:10, 20:25-28, 20:114, 23:118, 25:74, 28:24, 59:10
   Tanzil Quran Text, "quran-simple" (with its vowels and pause marks), https://tanzil.net, through api.alquran.cloud.
   Tanzil's terms: verbatim copying and distribution allowed, with the source (Tanzil Project, tanzil.net) indicated;
   the text may not be changed. Nothing is changed: a du'a inside a longer ayah is the ayah's own words from the first
   word of the du'a ("رَبَّنَا" / "رَبِّ", after "and they said:") to the ayah's end; several ayat are joined by a space.
   English: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall, "The Meaning of the Glorious Koran" (1930), edition "en.pickthall" of the same
   API: in the public domain (the author died in 1936; in the US, published 1930 + 95 years). Cut from its own
   "Our Lord!" / "My Lord!" to match the Arabic.

The anytime collections (added with the adhkar display), "daily: false" in the app:
- Remembrance (dhikr, 8): Hisn chapter 131 "the virtue of tasbih, tahmid, tahlil and takbir", items 274-277, 279, 280,
  282, 285 (muslim-data). Each is a hadith that carries a dhikr ("…مَنْ قَالَ: سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ فِي يَوْمٍ مِائَةَ مَرَّةٍ…"):
  the dhikr is the dataset's own words at fixed positions of its string (tools/adhkar_build.py prints every cut), the
  whole hadith stays as the virtue (Arabic and English), the count is the hadith's (100, 10), the English the
  dataset's quotation of the dhikr inside its translation.
- Seeking forgiveness (istighfar, 7): Sayyid al-Istighfar (me11) and "astaghfirullah wa atubu ilayh" ×100 (me33), the
  same items as in the morning adhkar; Hisn items 270 (cut from its hadith, ch. 130), 57 and 58 (before the salam,
  ch. 24), 216 (the end of a gathering, ch. 86), 283 (cut, ch. 131).
- Salawat (3): the two forms of the Ibrahimiyya, Hisn items 53 and 54 (ch. 23), with the virtue of items 239 and 241
  (ch. 108, "the virtue of the prayer upon the Prophet"); the du'a after the adhan, item 25 (ch. 15).
- Du'as from the Quran (15): above.
- Hard times (distress, 10): Hisn chapters 35 (anxiety and sorrow: 140, 141), 36 (distress: 142-145), 54 (a calamity:
  174), 44 (a difficult matter: 159), 42 (debt: 156), 47 (something disliked: 164).
- Everyday du'as (everyday, 21): fitrahive/dua-dhikr data/dua-dhikr/daily-dua/en.json (MIT, the same commit), with
  their transliteration and benefit: leaving home (2), the mosque (entering, leaving), wudu (before, after), eating
  (before, forgetting the basmala, after), breaking the fast, clothes (2), a vehicle, travel, rain (3), wind, the toilet
  (2); entering home is Hisn item 18 without its last words, an instruction ("ثُمَّ لِيُسَلِّمْ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ", then greet
  your family); the dataset's note after its translation is the virtue.
- Each everyday and hard-times du'a has its occasion: English written for the app ("Entering the mosque"), Arabic the
  name of its Hisn chapter in muslim-data (language "ar"), the tatweel (letter stretching, U+0640) removed.
- Not taken: the sneezing du'as and the resident's du'as for a traveller (fitrahive), Hisn's Hajj, funeral and
  sickness chapters, and its narrative items ("he used to…") whose dhikr is not separable without retyping.

Hisn al-Muslim itself: the author permits printing and distributing it free of charge; this app is free.


What the build changes (and nothing else)
------------------------------------------
- Whitespace trimmed; a trailing citation in the Arabic of the evening item me3 ("[البقرة 285 - 286]") moved out
  (it is a reference, not part of the dhikr).
- Before sleep: Hisn's one item "سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ (ثلاثاً وثلاثين) و الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (ثلاثاً وثلاثين) وَ اللَّهُ أَكْبَر (أربعاً
  وثلاثين)" is cut at its own count annotations into three counted items (33, 33, 34), the joining "و" dropped.
- Counts not in a dataset's count field come from the dataset's own text: sleep hm119 ×3 (Bukhari 5017: "he did that
  three times"; hisnmuslim.com gives 3), hm124 ×3 ("(Three times)"), hm72 ×10 ("Ten times after Maghrib and Fajr").
- After prayer, following Hisn al-Muslim: the three Quls once after every prayer, three times after Fajr and Maghrib
  (fitrahive says 3× and repeats the morning/evening virtue, both dropped); hm72 only after Fajr and Maghrib; the
  dua for beneficial knowledge (ap13) only after Fajr. Order as on Mawaqit's screen (core/Texts.kt): Ayat al-Kursi
  before the Quls; "Allahumma a'inni" (Hisn: end of the prayer) after "Allahumma anta as-salam", as Mawaqit shows it.
- English translations of chapter 1/29: a trailing note in parentheses ("(Whoever…)", "(Three times)") becomes the
  virtue/note field; for 2:285-286 the dataset repeats a second translation after the note: dropped.
- Short references ("Muslim 2723") extracted from the English source text; the full source is kept ("s", "sa").
- Tasbih, tahmid, takbir (ap5-7) get the source of the item that completes the hundred (ap8).
- UI languages other than English and Arabic show the English translation.
- References: fitrahive's "al-Bukhari" no longer becomes "al-Al-Bukhari"; a reference cut at "Vol." loses that word
  (the existing items are unchanged: the build checks the old 68 byte for byte).
- 130 items in all (128 KB, 36 KB gzipped).


Cross-check (tools/adhkar_crosscheck.py, letter skeleton: vowels, marks and alef forms ignored)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Against the Quran (Tanzil "simple-clean" text via api.alquran.cloud), Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim
(github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api, ara-bukhari / ara-muslim) and Mawaqit's own after-prayer screen (core/Texts.kt):
- Ayat al-Kursi (me2, ap9, hm120) = 2:255; al-Baqara 285-286 (me3, hm121) = 2:285-286; al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq,
  an-Nas (me4-6, ap10-12) = suras 112-114, whole and exact.
- me33 in Bukhari 6307; me34, me31, me7/me8 (first sentence), ap4 found in Sahih Muslim; hm125, hm1 in Bukhari 6324.
- Mawaqit (Texts.azkar): ap1, ap2, hm59, ap3, ap8, ap9 and the Quls found in its text.
- The anytime collections: hm274, 276, 277, 282, 283, 280, 58, 142, 174, 164 found in Sahih Muslim (by text); Abu
  Bakr's du'a hm57 too, around a note: Muslim 2705 reads "ظُلْمًا كَبِيرًا - وَقَالَ قُتَيْبَةُ كَثِيرًا -" and Hisn prints
  Qutayba's "كثيرا", so its two halves are checked in the same hadith. The 15 Quranic du'as are inside their ayat of
  Tanzil's quran-simple-clean, letter for letter.
- Known variants, kept as Hisn al-Muslim prints them: Sayyid al-Istighfar (me11) reads "وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي" in all three
  datasets, Bukhari 6306 "وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي"; "تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلاَلِ" (ap2, also Mawaqit) where Muslim 591 has
  "تَبَارَكْتَ ذَا الْجَلاَلِ".
